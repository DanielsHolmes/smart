# **Smart Investments** **Website Refresh — Complete Design Team Brief**

Standalone kickoff brief | September 2026

PURPOSE: This document is the complete starting brief for the website design team. Assume no prior briefing or context. It consolidates the client direction, content requirements, Tim’s three design mockup PDFs, and the latest website-refresh meeting notes. The mockups are directional references—not approved final layouts or final copy.

| CORE DIRECTION |
| :---- |
| Build one Smart Investments parent website with a clean Group Home that introduces Tim’s investment philosophy, values, history and ownership mindset, then routes visitors into two distinct but visually related platforms: (1) Commercial Real Estate and (2) Consumer Brands & Manufacturing. |

# **1\. Project Objective**

Refresh Smart Investments’ existing website so it reflects the scale, professionalism, history and long-term direction of the business. The new site must work as a credible corporate reference while clearly explaining that Smart Investments operates across two focused investment platforms.

* Establish Smart Investments first as the parent Canadian private holding/investment company.  
* Communicate Tim’s philosophy and values before presenting individual assets or companies.  
* Create two clear platform experiences without making them feel like unrelated websites.  
* Give investors, lenders, founders, brands, brokers, tenants, partners, advisors and prospective employees a fast path to the information relevant to them.  
* Use concise, factual, understated language. Avoid hype, guaranteed-return language, excessive financial claims and a residential-brokerage look.

# **9\. Visual Design Direction**

Tim’s three supplied mockup PDFs—Smart Investment Home, Smart Investment Commercial Real Estate Platform, and Smart Investment Consumer Brands & Manufacturing—are the PRIMARY visual references for the refresh. They communicate the desired hierarchy and tone, but are not locked layouts. The design team may refine layout, wording placement, interactions and responsive behavior.

| Element | Direction |
| :---- | :---- |
| Overall feel | Premium, modern, established, confident, understated and editorial. Institutional credibility without feeling cold or generic. |
| Palette | Gold \+ black are existing brand cues. Use white/off-white, charcoal/dark text and restrained warm accents. Avoid excessive gold, gradients and luxury clichés. |
| Typography — IMPORTANT | Font consistency was specifically raised in the meeting. Establish ONE controlled typography system across Group Home \+ both platforms. The mockups’ editorial serif-style headlines paired with clean sans-serif support copy are the starting reference. Define families, weights, sizes, line heights, labels, buttons/CTAs and mobile scaling globally. |
| Whitespace / layout | Generous whitespace, restrained grids, short copy blocks, strong editorial hierarchy, selective rules/dividers and clear section transitions. |
| Imagery | Prioritize authentic, high-quality property/company imagery. Avoid generic skyline, handshake or generic corporate stock photography. |
| Motion | Subtle and purposeful only. Avoid heavy animation or slow effects. |
| Platform differentiation | One parent visual system. CRE can be more institutional/property-led; Consumer Brands & Manufacturing can be more operator/brand-led. Do not create two unrelated visual identities. |
| Responsive | The two-platform choice and primary CTAs must remain obvious on mobile. Large display typography must scale cleanly. |

Secondary style references previously identified: KingSett Capital, Crestpoint Real Estate Investments, Dream, Slate Asset Management and Brookfield Properties. Use these only for cues around institutional presentation, property imagery and credibility—not for copying.

# **10\. Content Rules / Guardrails**

* Use concise, factual and credible language. Avoid overly promotional or speculative wording.  
* No guaranteed returns or unsupported performance claims.  
* Do not publish confidential investor reports, distributions, property-level financials or sensitive operating data on the public site.  
* All ownership descriptions, investment relationships, square footage, portfolio statistics, performance/value-creation metrics and partner/tenant/lender logos require factual approval.  
* Testimonials only if authentic, approved and appropriate.  
* Avoid excessive jargon, long dense text blocks, residential-agent styling, trendy effects that will date quickly, and generic stock imagery.  
* Final page-by-page copy can be refined in parallel with design; the current mockup copy is working copy unless specifically approved.
